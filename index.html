Verified & protected login

Passwords alone can be leaked, phished, or guessed. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds a dynamic second layer of defense by combining something you know (your password) with a time-sensitive cryptographic token generated from a shared secret key.

Using the open RFC 6238 (TOTP) standard, your secret key is hashed together with the current Unix timestamp every 30 seconds via HMAC-SHA1/256/512. Because both your browser and the target service share the same secret and clock, the code matches automatically without needing any network communication.

How to use 2FA Live Generator

    Your 6-digit token updates automatically every 30 seconds. Click Copy Code (or press Ctrl+C) and paste it to log in.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool safe to use?

Yes. All cryptographic HMAC calculations happen 100% client-side inside your browser's built-in Web Crypto API. No secret keys are ever transmitted over the network, and there are zero third-party ad scripts or analytics trackers on this page.

Can I use this offline?

Yes! Once loaded, you can turn off Wi-Fi or unplug your ethernet cable and continue generating accurate TOTP codes, scanning QR codes (via native BarcodeDetector), and exporting QR codes completely offline.

Why is my code being rejected?

TOTP relies on accurate time synchronization. If your generated code is rejected, ensure your computer or phone clock is set to Automatic date & time (synced via NTP), and verify that you copied the complete Base32 secret key without typos.

Where are my Recent Keys stored?

Recent keys are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage so you can quickly switch between accounts. Click Clear at any time to permanently wipe them from your browser.
